In Galician tradition, the Santa Compaña is a procession of souls that walks at night carrying candles. In Irish tradition, the cry of the banshee announces that someone in a family is about to die.

This essay will explore both figures with curiosity and care: where they come from, how they were told and what they reveal about the way rural Atlantic communities lived with death.
