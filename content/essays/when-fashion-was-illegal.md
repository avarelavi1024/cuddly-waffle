Today we choose what to wear with a freedom that would have seemed dangerous for most of history. In many European societies, laws told people which fabrics, colours and ornaments they were allowed to use according to their rank.

This essay will look at those sumptuary laws: why they existed, how people tried to escape them and what they say about the relationship between clothing, status and control.
