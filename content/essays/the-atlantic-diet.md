The Mediterranean diet has become one of the most famous ideas in nutrition. Yet the traditional way of eating in Galicia and northern Portugal, often called the Atlantic diet, has its own foods, rhythms and customs.

This essay will explain what the Atlantic diet is, how it differs from the Mediterranean model, what research says about it and why food culture matters as much as nutrients.
